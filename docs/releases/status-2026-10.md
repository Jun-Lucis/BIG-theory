# BIG Research Status — Through B41 (October 2026)

This is an **October 2026 repository-level research-status snapshot** for Boundary Information Geometry (BIG), extending the preceding `status-2026-09` snapshot through BIG-B41 and including the **EV6 external numerical holdout**. It is **not a new scientific paper** and does not replace the individual citable Zenodo publications.

## Programme developments

**B27–B36 — history, reconfiguration and readout.** Tests distinguish retained history from readable history, identify limits to geometry-only response-lineage transfer, and examine transverse suppression, reflection parity, complex frequency response and geometry-conditioned pulse timing. Positive, negative and prematurely closed tests are preserved.

**B37–B40 — relational timing and covariance.** Finite-model relative-lag diagnostics led to weighted measurement duality, information-metric drift, clock reparameterization and orientation-reversing covariance tests. B39 and B40 preserve their original formally failed predictions; newer, separately frozen tests support narrower forms of covariance without retrospectively changing failed verdicts.

**B41 — prospective trajectory-space response geometry.** In a finite three-dimensional periodic vector-vorticity response family, local parameter secants were used to make response-blind quantitative predictions at previously unevaluated parameter offsets. The full retained sequence is:

```text
P1: TRAJECTORY_RESPONSE_GEOMETRY_TRANSFER_PASS
    32/32 tangent predictions beat nearest-anchor baseline
    pooled weighted RMSE improvement: 97.7883%

P2: DIMENSIONLESS_REMAINDER_TRANSFER_FAIL
    frozen scalar second-order remainder: 19/24 cells
    within 20% quantitative mismatch; primary gates C and E FAIL

P2 post-hoc: failure localization, not a regrade
    five mismatching cells in two strongly anti-aligned
    tangent/acceleration families

P3: FULL_SECOND_ORDER_GEOMETRIC_REMAINDER_TRANSFER_PASS
    a separately frozen, no-fit sign-sensitive predictor
    on 48 genuinely fresh target integrations
    48/48 tangent advantages; 93.1041% pooled improvement
    24/24 cells within 20%; Spearman rho 0.997391
```

**Do not collapse this chain into a single PASS.** The formal P2 FAIL remains unchanged, and the post-hoc diagnostic does not have independent prospective force. The P3 non-gating *scalar* comparator also performed well (24/24 cells; median relative mismatch **0.6755%**, versus **0.6792%** for the full version), so general superiority or necessity of full direction-sensitive geometry has **not** been demonstrated.

**EV6 — limited external-dataset holdout.** An externally sourced particle-laden gravity-current dataset was used to test a frozen response-transfer rule on five previously held-out particle-volume-fraction conditions. The mapping-only EV6 v1.1 integrity repair was completed **before** outcome exposure, leaving the evaluator and scientific protocol unchanged. The formal result was `EV6_NUMERICAL_HOLDOUT_SUPPORTIVE`: median NRMSE **0.036538**, **5/5** conditions at NRMSE <= 0.15, **32.55%** pooled improvement versus the frozen nearest-condition baseline, and **5/5** baseline advantages. This is external-*data* validation, **not** an independently replicated experiment or confirmation of a unique BIG-specific mechanism.

## Methodological companion

The separate [Failure-Preserving AI-Assisted Research](https://github.com/Jun-Lucis/failure-preserving-research) repository treats BIG as a methodological case study, not as proof of the physical correctness of BIG. B41 adds a clearly documented failure -> post-hoc diagnosis -> separately frozen fresh-test chain. Its earlier frozen **B19–B40** quantitative lineage audit and historical statistics are **not retroactively recomputed** to include B41.

## Claim boundary

BIG remains an **exploratory mathematical and numerical programme**. The results are constrained to specific reduced systems, finite resolutions, frozen protocols and/or selected external data. They do **not** establish a single universal numerical boundary law, a general physical theory, emergent time, a continuum theorem, Navier–Stokes regularity, or a universal mechanism across physics, biology and AI.

Programme-level synthesis remains:

> BIG provides a common formal language for comparing system-dependent boundary geometry, response, history, branch structure and reconfiguration. The current evidence does not establish one universal numerical boundary law across the programme.
