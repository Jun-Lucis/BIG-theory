# BIG-B27 Research Status Update

**Date:** 2026-09-29  
**Programme status:** CLOSED

## Scope

B27 tested a narrow finite-model distinction between:

1. history-conditioned local response geometry at identical present state; and
2. invariance of that history-induced response geometry across controlled topology reconfiguration.

It did not test personal identity, consciousness, subjective continuity, AI personhood, or a universal law of individuality.

## B27.1 — History-conditioned response geometry

Formal verdict:

HISTORY_RESPONSE_GEOMETRY_PASS

Using the same saved present phi field, retained and erased history states produced different normalized local response geometry. All four prospectively held-out history-write angles exceeded the frozen structural threshold at both N=128 and N=160.

The zero-feedback control remained null.

Narrow result:

> Retained readable history can condition subsequent local response geometry in the tested reduced model, not merely its scalar gain.

## Integrated conclusion

B27 supports the distinction

\[
\boxed{
\text{history-conditioned response}
\neq
\text{reconfiguration-invariant response lineage}
}
\]

Within the tested finite model, present response depends on retained history, but topology reconfiguration substantially transforms the geometry of that history-conditioned response.

B27 therefore shifts the open question from **invariant lineage** to **predictable lineage transformation**.
