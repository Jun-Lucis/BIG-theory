## Programme-level retained conclusion

The current evidence supports BIG more strongly as a **common formal language for system-dependent boundary geometry, response, history, branch structure, and reconfiguration** than as one universal quantitative boundary law.

Positive local or family-restricted results remain positive. Transfer limits remain limits. A later series may seek transformation laws, branch-local invariants, piecewise normal forms, continuum control, or new external validation, but it must do so as new work.

Any reinterpretation must be clearly labeled as later analysis and must not overwrite the archived formal result.

## Handoff to the next B-series

The next B-series begins only after this closeout. It may inherit equations, datasets, definitions, or hypotheses from the closed programme, but it should:

1. state a new scientific question;
2. declare what prior information is known;
3. freeze claim-bearing criteria before new outcomes are inspected;
4. preserve implementation-invalid, null, inconclusive, and negative outcomes without post-hoc rescue;
5. write to a new output namespace and archive;
6. cite this closeout and the relevant source DOI records rather than editing their verdicts.
