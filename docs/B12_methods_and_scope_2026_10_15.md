## Exact finite-horizon implementation

Refined model: N=6 resonance modes, h in R^12, T=70, dt=0.02, 3500 integration steps; rho=0.90; amp_I=1.45; 33 sigma_R points from 0 to 0.160 in 0.005 increments; 180 trajectories each, 5940 rows. The seed is 12150000 + round(sigma_R*100000)*1000 + trajectory_index. Every saved refined seed matches this rule. Noise remains in d (sigma_d=0.015) and h (sigma_h=0.025), and initial conditions remain random even at sigma_R=0. Thus zero resonance noise is not a wholly deterministic run.

Proximity F_prox is sigmoid((3.6-d)/0.55). R_score is mean(R). G_R_open is sigmoid((R_score-0.58)/0.055). G_lock is exp(-(R_score-0.82)^2/0.24^2) times sigmoid((1.55-d)/0.25). The component ignition gate is sigmoid((R_i-0.18)/0.030), clipped in the sigmoid helper for numerical stability. Resonance drift uses A_R=1.50, H_R=0.58, B_R_cross=0.06 times R_i times the mean of other components, and explicit noise-dependent damping 2.8*sigma_R^2*R_i. Resonance noise is 0.55*common_normal + sqrt(1-0.55^2)*independent_normal, with sqrt(dt) scaling. R is clipped to [0,1.35]. The finite-noise curve therefore combines additive noise and an explicit sigma-dependent drift; this is not a demonstration that additive noise alone suffices.

Distance has an imposed incoming/outgoing protocol (-0.105 until t=36, +0.13 thereafter), resonance-mediated attraction/hold, repulsion and noise, with clipping to [0.35,9]. It is not a continuum boundary-field PDE or a verified trajectory handoff from B9/B10/B11. The energy in manuscript Eq.4 is schematic, not a complete derivation of every reduced drift.

## Ordered events, classifier and success

Hit is the first updated d<1.45. A lock step requires updated d<1.55, R_score>0.68 and G_lock>0.25. The first lock event requires at least 3.0 consecutive time units of lock steps. Strict final lock additionally requires the fraction of lock steps after hit >=0.40 and the fraction in the final 5.0-time-unit buffer >=0.35. Final full_success is strict_lock AND final h_outcome=='inheritance'. It is not just final similarity or an ever-observed inheritance flag. Every saved row in all five scans matches this Boolean identity.

Classifier order is annihilation, A-assimilation, B-assimilation, inheritance, emergent, mixed. The inheritance branch requires both sector memory cosines >0.62, sector norm balance >0.42 and overall similarity to the combined inheritance template >0.70, after excluding earlier branches. Assimilation uses parent-template similarity >0.84 plus the other sector norm <0.35; annihilation norm <0.22. Checking only the four inheritance inequalities incorrectly ignores 18 higher-priority assimilation outcomes across the archive (7 in the refined scan). Respecting source priority reproduces the recorded inheritance label in all 27500 rows from the saved scalar observables; missing full vectors/sim_E prevent a full independent reconstruction of every other class.

The historical inherited_during_lock flag is latched when inheritance is observed after first_lock_event; its code does not require is_lock_step to be true at that particular instant. It does not prove inheritance first arose only after locking. Twelve refined rows differ between strict_lock AND that latched flag and the final full_success field. The original labels are retained. All recorded refined success times are present and ordered hit <= lock <= recorded inheritance, but this does not establish causation or exclude an earlier inheritance state.

Hidden-depth mobility is 0.01+1.15*G_lock and the inheritance well amplitude is amp_I*G_lock, continuously gated, without a hard first-lock switch. The energy uses four Gaussian wells, width 0.38, weak quadratic/quartic confinement and folding conflict 2*(1-rho)*norm(h_A)^2*norm(h_B)^2. Since the gate and external protocol vary, endpoint energy drops are saved finite-horizon diagnostics, not a conservation law, thermodynamic yield or total-system Lyapunov proof.

Update order matters: gates are evaluated before R/d updates, hit/lock bookkeeping then uses updated d and pre-update gates, and h is updated afterwards. Decimated trace rows mix these explicitly recorded stages. Each representative trace has 350 rows (stride 10, displayed t=0 to 69.8), not all 3500 steps or full R/h vectors. Recomputing strict events from decimated rows alone is not valid.
