# BIG-B28–B29 — Limits of Response-Lineage Transport and Predictive Readability

**Integrated working paper:** *Limits of Response-Lineage Transport and Predictive Readability in a Memory-Bearing Boundary Model: Prospective BIG-B28-B29 Tests*

**Subtitle:** *Geometry-Only Split Transport, Frozen Stop Rules, and a Training-Domain Readability Boundary*

**Version:** v1.0  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23050390

## Scope

This integrated record consolidates already-completed BIG-B28 and BIG-B29 prospective programmes in a finite memory-bearing boundary model. It introduces no new trajectory, parameter scan, model fit, threshold selection, or retrospective regrading.

The immediate background is BIG-B27: retained readable history changed local response geometry at identical present state (`HISTORY_RESPONSE_GEOMETRY_PASS`), while the simplest frozen reconfiguration-covariance hypothesis failed (`RECONFIGURATION_COVARIANCE_FAIL`).

## B28 retained result

B28 froze a new geometry-only, component-resolved 1-to-2 split transport rather than weakening the B27 criterion.

Fresh B28.1 split targets:

[
\delta_{\rm post}=5.3,\ 5.6,\ 5.9.
]

All three were numerically valid. The primary lineage-transport defects were approximately

[
0.1533434,quad 0.1524679,quad 0.1564395,
]

well above the frozen PASS threshold (0.009) and PARTIAL ceiling (0.027).

**Formal verdict:** `GEOMETRY_CONDITIONED_LINEAGE_TRANSPORT_FAIL`

Under the frozen stop rule, B28.2 was not opened.

## B29 retained result

B29 asked whether retained-history information could add prospective predictive information beyond instantaneous geometry without relying on the failed B28 transport map.

The response-blind design froze 36 cases with a 27-training / 9-held-out split. B29-T1 evaluated only the 27 training cases. Eighteen were valid:

- relative-write 25°: 9/9 valid;
- relative-write 55°: 9/9 valid;
- relative-write 85°: 0/9 valid under the inherited response-readability gate.

**Terminal training status:** `T1_valid=False`

No held-out prediction set was frozen, no held-out post-response was evaluated, B29.1 was never opened, and no prospective held-out predictive verdict exists for M0, M1, or M2.

The retained interpretation is therefore a **training-domain readability boundary**, not a predictive-performance FAIL.

## Claim boundary

The integrated paper supports only the following limited conclusions:

- the tested geometry-only lineage-transport map failed prospectively under its frozen tolerance;
- the subsequent B29 programme stopped before held-out prediction because the frozen training domain was not uniformly response-readable.

It does **not** establish that all transport maps fail, that retained history is intrinsically untransportable, or that 85°/90° is a universal physical node. It makes no claim about personal identity, consciousness, AI personhood, biological inheritance, or a continuum topology-change theorem.
