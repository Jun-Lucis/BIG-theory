## Mathematical scope and restrictions

The bounded-domain, two-dimensional positive-flux prototype considered is

```text
u_t = div[(u^2 + epsilon) grad(u) + 4 gamma |grad(u)|^2 grad(u)]
      - mu u + S
```

with natural no-flux Neumann boundary condition, nonnegative bounded initial data and source, `gamma > 0`, `epsilon >= 0`, `mu >= 0`, and the stated variational solution class. The manuscript documents a conditional bounded weak-existence argument, **same-coefficient** `L^1` contraction/comparison/uniqueness, fixed-`gamma` `epsilon -> 0` and simultaneous `(gamma,epsilon) -> (0,0)` limits toward a Neumann porous-medium model.

A constant initial field and spatially uniform source yield a spatially uniform solution independent of `gamma` and `epsilon`: *forward uniqueness does not imply coefficient identifiability*. The positive all-domain Gaussian source discussed in the B4 numerical setting also precludes interpreting an apparent finite-grid near-two contour as a mathematical proof of a positive-time exact-zero support boundary.

These claims **do not establish** well-posedness for historically printed negative-quartic-flux equations, a universal BIG PDE, a new general PDE method, a rigorous compacton for the tested positive-source case, or BIG-specific added physical predictive value. The positive-flux version-specific B4 corrections remain separate from original negative-flux B3.1/B4 or B5/B7/B8 operators. Standard PDE literature is cited as prior art. No independent external mathematical peer review or physical calibration is claimed.
